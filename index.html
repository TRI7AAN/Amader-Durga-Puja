Pandal-hopping wisdom

Puja Tips

🌅

Go early or late

Saptami–Ashtami evenings are heaviest. Mornings and post-10pm nights are kinder for walking.

👟

Wear walking shoes

Trails run 7–8 km. Comfortable footwear, water, and a small bag for bhog coupons.

🛺

Keep auto change ready

Shared autos take ₹10–12 exact cash or UPI. Board at the marked stands, not mid-road.

📱

Save battery & data

Keep the trail tab open — progress is remembered. “Leg 1 / Leg 2” buttons open GPS instantly.
